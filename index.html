    How to use Fabrica

    Add fabric

    • Screenshot to clipboard (⌘⇧⌃4 on a Mac), then ⌘V.
    • Or drop picture files on the board, or use Add pictures.
    • Pictures keep their on-screen size, so screenshots taken at the same zoom stay comparable.

    Match pattern sizes

    • Select a sample and type its real width, or use Measure (M): drag across a ruler or one pattern repeat and type the length.
    • Measured samples are shown at real size and linked: resize one and all linked samples follow, keeping their proportions.
    • You can also link samples you matched by eye, or select several and resize them together.

    Stitches

    • Stitch (S): drag on a sample. Hold ⇧ for a straight line.
    • Stitches stick to their sample and move, turn and resize with it.
    • Click a stitch with the Stitch tool (or double-click it with Select) to change it. Edit these stitches on a sample selects all of its stitches.

    Thread colours

    • Pick colour (I, or ⌥-click while stitching): click a fabric to see threads that blend in, tone on tone, contrast or harmonise.
    • The round rainbow button opens a colour wheel for any colour.

    Saving

    Your board is saved in this browser automatically. Use Save to keep a copy as a file (pictures included) and Open to load it again.

    Thread colours: Mettler Metrosene all-purpose sewing thread. Numbers and names are from Mettler’s website; screen colours are estimated from Mettler’s swatch photos, so they are approximate.
